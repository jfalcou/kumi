Bibliography

These texts ground the terminology of the Glossary, from the textbooks to the original papers, and its notations of products and sums follow them together with the standard mathematical notation for sets and tuples. The terms of the C++ language are defined on the C++ Vocabulary page, and the terms specific to the library on the Nomenclature page.

Primary Texts

  • Serge Abiteboul, Richard Hull, and Victor Vianu, Foundations of Databases, Addison-Wesley, 1995, ISBN 978-0-201-53771-0, website.
  • Henk Barendregt, The Lambda Calculus: Its Syntax and Semantics, revised edition, Studies in Logic and the Foundations of Mathematics 103, North-Holland, 1984, ISBN 978-0-444-87508-2.
  • Henk Barendregt, Lambda calculi with types, in S. Abramsky, D. M. Gabbay, and T. S. E. Maibaum (eds.), Handbook of Logic in Computer Science 2, Oxford University Press, 1992, DOI:10.1093/oso/9780198537618.003.0002.
  • Robert Harper, Practical Foundations for Programming Languages, 2nd edition, Cambridge University Press, 2016, DOI:10.1017/CBO9781316576892.
  • Benjamin C. Pierce, Types and Programming Languages, MIT Press, 2002, website.
  • Wikipedia contributors, Type theory, Wikipedia, The Free Encyclopedia, website.

The Curry-Howard Correspondence

  • Haskell B. Curry, Functionality in Combinatory Logic, Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences 20(11), 1934, pp. 584-590, DOI:10.1073/pnas.20.11.584.
  • Timothy G. Griffin, A formulae-as-type notion of control, in Proceedings of the 17th ACM SIGPLAN-SIGACT Symposium on Principles of Programming Languages, POPL '90, 1990, pp. 47-58, DOI:10.1145/96709.96714.
  • William A. Howard, The formulae-as-types notion of construction, in J. P. Seldin and J. R. Hindley (eds.), To H. B. Curry: Essays on Combinatory Logic, Lambda Calculus and Formalism, Academic Press, 1980, pp. 479-490, PDF.
  • Philip Wadler, Propositions as Types, Communications of the ACM 58(12), 2015, pp. 75-84, DOI:10.1145/2699407, PDF.
